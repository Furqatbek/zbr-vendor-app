ZBR Owner ("the app") is the restaurant partner application of
[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us"), operator of the ZBR delivery platform. This
policy explains what the app collects, why, and what rights you have.

The app is for restaurants that have partnered with ZBR. It is not a consumer
food ordering app, and accounts are issued by us rather than created in the app.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** Your name, email address, and phone number, used to
authenticate you and identify your restaurant.

**Restaurant information.** The name, description, address, opening hours,
delivery settings, menu, and pricing you enter.

**Location.** If you tap "detect location" while setting your restaurant's
address, the app reads your device's location once to fill in the coordinates.
This happens **only** when you actively request it, only while the app is open,
and the app **never** collects location in the background. You may skip this and
enter or pick coordinates manually.

**Photos.** Images you choose for menu items or your profile. The app opens your
device's photo picker and receives only the images you select — it does not have
access to the rest of your photo library.

**Device identifier for notifications.** A push token issued by Google (FCM) or
Apple (APNs), used solely to deliver order notifications to your device.

**Integration credentials.** If you connect a Restos POS system, its address and
API key. The API key is stored in your device's secure hardware-backed keystore.

We do **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, microphone or
camera input, browsing history, or health data. **The app contains no advertising
and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.**

## 2. Information shown but not collected

The app displays customer names, delivery addresses, phone numbers and order
contents so you can fulfil orders, along with your revenue and payout figures.
This information is sent from our servers to your device for display. The app
does not gather it from your device and does not transmit it anywhere else.

You may only use customer information to fulfil the order it belongs to.

## 3. How we use information

- To authenticate you and keep you signed in
- To show and let you manage incoming orders
- To publish your restaurant, menu and location to customers on the ZBR platform
- To send order notifications
- To calculate and report your revenue and payouts
- To provide support and investigate problems

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for
advertising or profiling.

## 4. How information is stored and protected

- All communication uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). Unencrypted
  connections are blocked in release builds.
- Authentication tokens and integration API keys are stored in the device's
  secure keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), not in plain storage.
- Preferences such as language and your selected restaurant are stored
  unencrypted on the device and are removed when you sign out or uninstall.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your
information.

## 5. Sharing

We share information only with:

- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** and **Apple Push Notification service** —
  to deliver notifications to your device.
- **Service providers** who host and operate the platform on our behalf, bound
  by confidentiality obligations.
- **Authorities**, where required by law.

Notification delivery is handled by Google and Apple under their own privacy
policies.

## 7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal
information, and you may withdraw consent for optional data such as location.

**To request account and data deletion**, contact **[DELETION REQUEST EMAIL]**
or use **[DELETION REQUEST URL]**. We will respond within [NUMBER] days.
Some records may be retained where law requires it; we will tell you if so.

Because accounts are issued by ZBR to partner restaurants, deleting your account
also ends your ability to receive orders through the platform.

You can revoke notification, location, and photo permissions at any time in your
device settings. Revoking notifications means you will stop receiving order
alerts.
