Privacy Policy — ZBR Owner

Last updated: [DATE]

ZBR Owner ("the app") is the restaurant partner application of [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us"), operator of the ZBR delivery platform. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what rights you have.

The app is for restaurants that have partnered with ZBR. It is not a consumer food ordering app, and accounts are issued by us rather than created in the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your name, email address, and phone number, used to authenticate you and identify your restaurant.

Restaurant information. The name, description, address, opening hours, delivery settings, menu, and pricing you enter.

Location. If you tap "detect location" while setting your restaurant's address, the app reads your device's location once to fill in the coordinates. This happens only when you actively request it, only while the app is open, and the app never collects location in the background. You may skip this and enter or pick coordinates manually.

Photos. Images you choose for menu items or your profile. The app opens your device's photo picker and receives only the images you select — it does not have access to the rest of your photo library.

Device identifier for notifications. A push token issued by Google (FCM) or Apple (APNs), used solely to deliver order notifications to your device.

Integration credentials. If you connect a Restos POS system, its address and API key. The API key is stored in your device's secure hardware-backed keystore.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, microphone or camera input, browsing history, or health data. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Information shown but not collected

The app displays customer names, delivery addresses, phone numbers and order contents so you can fulfil orders, along with your revenue and payout figures. This information is sent from our servers to your device for display. The app does not gather it from your device and does not transmit it anywhere else.

You may only use customer information to fulfil the order it belongs to.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in
  • To show and let you manage incoming orders
  • To publish your restaurant, menu and location to customers on the ZBR platform
  • To send order notifications
  • To calculate and report your revenue and payouts
  • To provide support and investigate problems

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. How information is stored and protected

  • All communication uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). Unencrypted connections are blocked in release builds.
  • Authentication tokens and integration API keys are stored in the device's secure keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), not in plain storage.
  • Preferences such as language and your selected restaurant are stored unencrypted on the device and are removed when you sign out or uninstall.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Sharing

We share information only with:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Service providers who host and operate the platform on our behalf, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

Notification delivery is handled by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies.

6. Retention

We keep account and restaurant information for as long as your partnership with ZBR is active. Order and financial records are retained for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. "as long as required by applicable accounting and tax law"]. Push tokens are deleted when you sign out or the token becomes invalid.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent for optional data such as location.

To delete your account and data, use the app: open More → Delete account (also reachable from More → About → Account). The screen lists exactly what is removed, asks you to confirm by typing your email address, and deletes the account directly — you do not need to contact us first. If you cannot sign in, write to [DELETION REQUEST EMAIL] and we will respond within [NUMBER] days.

Deleting your account:

  • stops orders reaching you immediately, and removes your restaurant from the ZBR app that customers use;
  • revokes your sign-in credentials and deletes the push tokens for your devices, so notifications stop;
  • removes or anonymises your name, email address, phone number and staff records.

Financial and fiscal records that accounting and tax law require us to keep are retained for the period in section 6, but are no longer linked to an account you can sign in to. This cannot be undone.

You can revoke notification, location, and photo permissions at any time in your device settings. Revoking notifications means you will stop receiving order alerts.
